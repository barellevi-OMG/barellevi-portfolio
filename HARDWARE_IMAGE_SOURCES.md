All remote product photos remain the property of their rights holders. Source attribution does not automatically grant publication rights. A verified license or authorized replacement photography may be needed for long-term commercial use. The page uses locally authored SVG fallback images when remote photos cannot be loaded.

Unverified hardware model numbers are not represented as facts.
