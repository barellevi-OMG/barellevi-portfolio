02 / ENGINEERING PROCESS

From question
to evidence.

Follow a staged work method and distinguish what was verified in historical records from what remains open.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Reproduce the reported throughput issue.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Separate direct baseline tests from tests through intermediary components.

  3. 03

    Isolate

    Inspect physical negotiation and single-variable configuration changes.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Retest the same path and record residual variance without declaring unverified success.
